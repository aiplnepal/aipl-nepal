- **Description:** Implements SFMS using Internet of Things (IoT) technology.
- **Benefits:** Improves agricultural productivity, optimizes resource utilization, reduces operational costs, and minimizes environmental impact.
- **Customer Outcome:**

## 8. IoT / SMART AGRICULTURE
- **Smart Irrigation System:** **[CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED]** Automatically controls the water amount based on soil moisture, weather, and crop condition to save water and increase production.
- **Soil Quality Monitoring:** **[CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED]** IoT sensors measure soil moisture, pH level, temperature, and nutrient content, providing farmers with recommendations via SMS on mobile phones to supply required fertilizers/water at the right time.
- **Environmental Monitoring:** **[CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED]** Sensors collect data on temperature, humidity, wind, and rainfall to detect pests/diseases early and send timely recommendations via SMS.
- **Crop Health Monitoring:** **[CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED]** IoT cameras and sensors monitor crop conditions. Damaged or diseased crops can be detected immediately for prompt treatment.
- **Livestock Monitoring:** **[CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED]** Monitors the activity, health, and feeding status of livestock (e.g., cows, goats) so diseases can be identified and treated on time.
- **Data Analytics Platform:** Software analyzes collected data to provide recommendations to farmers.
- **Hardware Used:** Sensors (moisture, temperature, weather), Drones (for field monitoring and applying fertilizers/pesticides), and Smart Devices (smart pumps, remote-controlled tractors).
